## Tone and style

- **Tone:** Professional, technical but accessible
- **Focus:** Architecture, patterns, and technical decisions
- **Avoid:** Unverifiable metrics, commercial claims, exaggerated outcomes
- **Prefer:** System descriptions, architectural patterns, technology choices,
  implementation details

---

## Differentiators

1. **Full-stack automation:** Not just workflows — backend APIs, database design,
   deployment infrastructure, and CI/CD pipelines
2. **Bilingual by design:** Systems handle EN/ES natively — not translation, architecture
3. **Human-in-the-loop:** Automate where it adds value, keep human judgment where it matters
4. **Security by default:** Input sanitization at gateway, zero secrets in code,
   database-level enforcement
5. **Documented architecture:** Every project includes READMEs, architecture diagrams,
   and technical decision records

---

## Professional objectives

1. Position as an AI Automation Engineer building end-to-end production systems
2. Demonstrate complete system design — not just isolated workflows
3. Attract opportunities in AI automation, workflow engineering, and integration roles
4. Maintain a living portfolio that evolves with each new project

## Content Strategy

### Voice
- First-person, narrative ("I built...", "Here's how...")
- Technical but accessible
- No hype or buzzwords without context
- Honest about trade-offs and challenges

### Hashtag strategy
- Primary (every post): #AIAutomation #n8n #WorkflowAutomation
- Secondary (pick 1-2): #APIIntegration #CRMAutomation #VoiceAI #Webhooks
Maximum 3 per post.

### Verification
Every post is verified against the Content Verification Rules in content-strategy.md
before publishing. No invented clients, metrics, or outcomes.

## Networking Voice

### Professional principles
- Connect with intent, not volume
- Add value before asking for anything
- Technical conversations over social networking
- Consistency over intensity

### Tone in messages
- Professional but approachable
- Specific to the person (no templates copied without personalization)
- Refer to portfolio as evidence, not marketing
- No unsolicited pitches or service offers

### Commenting style
- Add technical perspective or experience
- Reference specific projects when relevant
- Ask thoughtful questions about trade-offs
- No generic praise ("Great post!")
